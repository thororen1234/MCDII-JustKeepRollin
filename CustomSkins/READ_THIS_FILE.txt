CUSTOM SKINS
============

Wear your own skins in Minecraft Dungeons II, and switch them while you play!

Put your skins in the Skins folder (see INSTALL) as 1.png, 2.png, 3.png... up to 20.png. They must be in the game's skin format: convert Java skins first with https://ewanhowell.com/tools/dungeons2skinconverter. The face the converter keeps (with its eyes and mouth) is put on your character's head for you.

To pick a skin, open your inventory and go to Collectibles, where the capes are: the Custom Skins row there has a button for each skin, and one for the game's skin. Your choice is remembered.

Keys:
    F6  Wear the next skin (after the last one, the game's skin again)
    F7  Read the skin files again, to see changes you just saved in your image editor
    F8  Save the skin picked in the game's menu as a PNG in Skins\_game, to start your own from
    F9  Write what the mod knows about your character to the log and show it on screen. If something doesn't work, press F9 in game, press Copy and send the log.

A skin can have its own <number>_MRES.png next to it (red metallic, green roughness, blue glow, alpha subsurface). Without one, it gets a plain one.

Only you can see your custom skin. Everyone else sees the skin you picked in the game's own menu, so pick the one you would like them to see.

REQUIREMENTS
------------

- Blueprint Loader, which must also be installed
https://www.nexusmods.com/minecraftdungeons2/mods/2

INSTALL
-------

1. Close the game, and fully close the Minecraft Launcher if you use it.

2. Open your Minecraft Dungeons II Paks folder.
    Steam: Right click the game in your library, then Manage > Browse local files, then open Dungeons\Content\Paks
    Minecraft Launcher: C:\XboxGames\Minecraft Dungeons II\Content\Dungeons\Content\Paks

3. Create a folder called ~mods inside the Paks folder if there is not one already.

4. Extract this zip into ~mods, so the files end up in: Paks\~mods\CustomSkins

5. Create a folder called Skins inside Paks\~mods\CustomSkins and put your skins there.

UNINSTALL
---------

Delete the CustomSkins folder from ~mods.
